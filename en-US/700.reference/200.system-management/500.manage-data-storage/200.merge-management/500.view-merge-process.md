# View major compaction information

After initiating a major compaction, you can view the compaction information through views.

The process of a major compaction can be viewed through views. Generally, the duration of a major compaction depends on the amount of data changed between two major compactions. A larger amount of data change between two major compactions results in a longer compaction time. Additionally, factors such as the number of threads used for the compaction and the cluster load during the compaction also affect the duration.

## View major compaction information of all tenants in the system tenant

You can query views in the system tenant to view the tenant-level and partition-level major compaction information of all tenants.

1. Log in to the `sys` tenant of the cluster as the `root` user.
