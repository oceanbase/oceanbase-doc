# Package management

Click **System Configuration** > **Package Management** in the left-side navigation pane to go to the **Packages** page. This topic describes how to view and upload packages in the obshell Dashboard.

## View packages

The **Packages** page displays the **Package Name**, **Version**, **Architecture**, **Size (MB)**, **MD5**, and **Uploaded At** of the packages uploaded to the obshell Dashboard.

You can click the <span style="vertical-align: middle;"><img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/observer-enterprise/V4.3.5/700.reference/1500.Components-and-Tools/100.obshell/500.icon/100.ob-dashboard-tenant-02.png" alt="Filter" style="height: 20px;"></span> icon next to **Version** and **Architecture** to filter packages by version and architecture. You can also click the <span style="vertical-align: middle;"><img src="https://obbusiness-private.oss-cn-shanghai.aliyuncs.com/doc/img/observer-enterprise/V4.3.5/700.reference/1500.Components-and-Tools/100.obshell/500.icon/100.ob-dashboard-tenant-03.png" alt="Sort" style="height: 20px;"></span> icon next to **Package Name** and **Uploaded At** to sort packages in ascending or descending order. If you have uploaded too many packages, you can search for a specific package in the search box in the upper-left corner of the page.

## Upload a package

Click **Upload Package** in the upper-right corner of the **Packages** page and click **Select Package** to upload a package from your local device. If no package is available on your local device, you can click **RPM Package Download Address** to download the required package from the open-source mirror site.
